About the Book

What Happens After
the Defence?

Research Creates Knowledge. Purpose Turns It Into Impact.”

From Thesis to Transformation is a practical, hopeful guide for everyone who has poured years into a thesis and wondered what happens next. It reframes the thesis not as a final document, but as raw material — for a book readers can hold, a brand people recognise, and a career that lasts.

Part manifesto, part playbook, the book walks you through the questions that matter after submission: Who needs this knowledge? What form should it take next? And how do you keep your research working long after the defence is over?

Written for
  • Undergraduates & postgraduates finishing their research
  • Academics & supervisors guiding the next cohort
  • Professionals turning expertise into influence

Inside the Book

What You’ll Find
Between the Pages

From Thesis to Transformation moves through five parts — each one turning a stage of the research journey into a concrete next step, drawn from Felicia’s path through IT Business, Petroleum Accounting and Finance, an MPhil in Accounting and an ongoing PhD.

Part I

Beyond the Defence

Why a thesis is raw material, not a finished endpoint — and how to see your own research through the lens of publication, influence and opportunity instead of submission.

Part II

From Examiners to Readers

Reshaping academic argument into a manuscript people choose to read: structure, voice, storytelling with data, and the discipline of writing for a public without losing rigour.

Part III

Building a Research Brand

Making expertise visible — the talks, articles and conversations that carry accounting and finance research out of the library and into boardrooms, policy debates and classrooms.

Part IV

Knowledge into Career

Translating depth into livelihood: academic pathways, consulting, entrepreneurship and the Ghana-focused questions of accountability, sustainability and national development.

Part V

Your Next Chapter

A practical playbook for what to do on Monday morning — the ninety-day plan, the publishing routes, and how to keep your research working long after the celebration ends.

Plus

Tools & Reflections

Worksheets, checklists and candid reflections from balancing a PhD, business and family — the honest companion to the strategy.

About the Author

The Voice Behind
the Book

Felicia Awura Ama Twumwaa Odei Benefo

Felicia Awura Ama Twumwaa Odei Benefo is an accounting scholar, researcher, entrepreneur and emerging academic professional with a strong interest in research, accountability, sustainability and the role of knowledge in national development.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in IT Business, a Master of Science in Petroleum Accounting and Finance, and an MPhil in Accounting, and is currently pursuing a PhD in Accounting. Her academic journey has strengthened her conviction that research should not end with a thesis or dissertation but should contribute to meaningful conversations, professional opportunities and solutions to real-world problems.

Beyond academia, Felicia is an entrepreneur who understands the challenges of building a career while pursuing advanced education. Her experiences across research, business and professional development inform her belief that academic knowledge can become a powerful personal and professional asset when intentionally developed.

Through this book, she seeks to encourage a new generation of researchers to ask a bigger question:

“What can my research become beyond the thesis?”

From Thesis to Transformation is her invitation to researchers to think beyond completion and begin thinking about publication, influence, visibility, opportunity and impact.

Your thesis is not the end of your research journey.
It may be the beginning of your next chapter.
