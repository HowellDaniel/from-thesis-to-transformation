About the Book

What Happens After
the Defence?

Research Creates Knowledge. Purpose Turns It Into Impact.”

From Thesis to Transformation is a practical, hopeful guide for everyone who has poured years into a thesis and wondered what happens next. It reframes the thesis not as a final document, but as raw material — for a book readers can hold, a brand people recognise, and a career that lasts.

Part manifesto, part playbook, the book walks you through the questions that matter after submission: Who needs this knowledge? What form should it take next? And how do you keep your research working long after the defence is over?

Written for
  • Undergraduates & postgraduates finishing their research
  • Academics & supervisors guiding the next cohort
  • Professionals turning expertise into influence

About the Author

The Voice Behind
the Book

Felicia Awura Ama Twumwaa Odei Benefo

Felicia Awura Ama Twumwaa Odei Benefo is a scholar, researcher, entrepreneur and finance professional with a strong interest in research sustainability and the role of knowledge in development.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in Development Studies, a Master of Science in Petroleum Accounting and Finance, and postgraduate training in Accounting, and is currently pursuing further research studies. Her academic journey has strengthened her conviction that research should not end with a thesis on a shelf — it should contribute to meaningful conversations, opportunities and solutions to real-world problems.

Beyond academia, Felicia is an entrepreneur who knows first-hand the challenges of building a career with education. Her experiences across industries and professional development spaces inform her belief that knowledge can become a powerful personal asset when intentionally developed.

“What can my research become beyond the thesis? From Thesis to Transformation is an invitation to rethink research beyond completion and publication — into opportunity and impact.”

Your thesis is not the end of your research journey. It may be the beginning of its most important chapter.
