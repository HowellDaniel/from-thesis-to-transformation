About the Book

What happens after the defence?

Research creates knowledge. Purpose turns it into impact.

From Thesis to Transformation is a practical guide for anyone wondering what can happen after years of research. It shows how academic work can become a book, a public voice, a career asset and a contribution to real-world conversations.

Written for
  • Students completing research
  • Academics and supervisors
  • Professionals turning expertise into influence

About the Author

The voice behind the book

Felicia Awura Ama Twumwaa Odei Benefo

Felicia is an accounting scholar, researcher, entrepreneur and emerging academic professional with an interest in research, accountability, sustainability and the role of knowledge in national development.

She holds a BSc in IT Business, an MSc in Petroleum Accounting and Finance and an MPhil in Accounting, and is pursuing a PhD in Accounting. Her work is grounded in the belief that research should continue beyond the thesis and contribute to meaningful conversations and practical solutions.

“What can my research become beyond the thesis?”

From Thesis to Transformation is an invitation to think beyond completion and toward publication, visibility, opportunity and impact.
