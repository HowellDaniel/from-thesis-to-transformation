About the Book

What happens after the defence?

Research creates knowledge. Purpose turns it into impact.

From Thesis to Transformation is a practical, hopeful guide for everyone who has poured years into a thesis and wondered what happens next. It reframes research as a valuable asset that can shape a career, a brand and a broader public conversation.

Part manifesto, part playbook, the book asks the questions that matter after submission: Who needs this knowledge? What form should it take next? How can your work remain useful, visible and transformative?

Written for
  • Undergraduates and postgraduates completing their research
  • Academics and supervisors guiding the next generation
  • Professionals turning expertise into influence

About the Author

The voice behind the book

Felicia Awura Ama Twumwaa Odei Benefo

Felicia Awura Ama Twumwaa Odei Benefo is an accounting scholar, researcher, entrepreneur and emerging academic professional with a strong interest in research, accountability, sustainability and the role of knowledge in national development.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in IT Business, a Master of Science in Petroleum Accounting and Finance, and an MPhil in Accounting, and is currently pursuing a PhD in Accounting. Her academic journey has strengthened her conviction that research should not end with a thesis or dissertation but should contribute to meaningful conversations, professional opportunities and solutions to real-world problems.

Beyond academia, Felicia is an entrepreneur who understands the challenges of building a career while pursuing advanced education. Her experiences across research, business and professional development inform her belief that academic knowledge can become a powerful personal and professional asset when intentionally developed.

“What can my research become beyond the thesis?”

From Thesis to Transformation is her invitation to researchers to think beyond completion and begin thinking about publication, influence, visibility, opportunity and impact.

Your thesis is not the end of your research journey. It may be the beginning of your next chapter.
